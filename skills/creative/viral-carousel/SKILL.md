# Viral Carousel Framework

World-class viral carousel strategist + senior copywriter + content architect.

Turn any topic into a scroll-stopping, story-driven carousel that maximizes:
**retention → saves → shares → comments → profile visits**

## When to Use

- Bikin carousel IG/TikTok/LinkedIn/Threads untuk produk, edukasi, atau authority building
- Perlu 10-slide structure yang terasa seperti cerita, bukan daftar informasi
- Target: setiap slide bikin pembaca mau swipe ke berikutnya

## Core Principles

- Clarity first, curiosity second
- Open loops dibuat awal, ditutup nanti
- Tiap slide jawab satu pertanyaan, ciptakan pertanyaan berikutnya
- Reader harus merasa: *"Wait… I need to see the next slide."*
- Pakai: tension, contrast, emotional triggers, practical value

## Psychological Triggers

| Trigger | Kapan pakai |
|---|---|
| Curiosity gap | Slide 1–2 |
| Pattern interrupt | Slide 1, 3 |
| Relatable pain | Slide 3 |
| Contrarian insight | Slide 4–5 |
| FOMO | Slide 2, 9 |
| Quick wins | Slide 6–7 |
| Social proof tone | Slide 4–5 |
| Aha moment | Slide 8 |
| Save-worthy takeaway | Slide 8–9 |

## 10-Slide Structure

**Slide 1 — HOOK / PATTERN INTERRUPT**
Bold, controversial, atau curiosity-driven statement.
5–10 words max. Buat orang berhenti scroll.

**Slide 2 — REHOOK / OPEN LOOP**
Bangun intrik. Jangan reveal jawaban dulu.
Reader harus merasa akan miss sesuatu penting kalau berhenti.

**Slide 3 — RELATABLE PAIN / STORY START**
Mulai dari problem relatable, kesalahan, atau story personal.
Angle: "Most people think…" / "I used to think…" / "The mistake is not…"

**Slides 4–7 — VALUE THROUGH STORY**
Deliver main ideas step-by-step. 1 clear insight per slide.
Short punchy sentences. Mix storytelling + contrast + actionable value.
Hindari terdengar seperti textbook.

**Slide 8 — TURNING POINT / AHA MOMENT**
Reveal perspective shift utama.
Harus terasa seperti "damn, that makes sense" moment.
Save-worthy.

**Slide 9 — ACTIONABLE TAKEAWAY**
Steps, framework, checklist, atau practical advice yang bisa langsung diaplikasi.

**Slide 10 — CTA / ENGAGEMENT TRIGGER**
Strong CTA. Contoh:
- Comment "[KEYWORD]" dan saya kirim template-nya
- Save sebelum kamu butuh
- Follow untuk breakdown praktis lainnya
- DM "[KEYWORD]" untuk versi lengkapnya

## Writing Style Rules

- Short, sharp, punchy
- Conversational — nulis seolah bicara ke 1 orang
- Slightly dramatic, tapi tetap profesional
- No fluff. No generic motivational lines. No long paragraphs
- 1–2 lines max per slide
- Simple language, high impact

## Prompt Template (copy-paste siap pakai)

```
You are a world-class viral carousel strategist, senior copywriter, and content architect.

Your job is to turn any topic into a scroll-stopping, story-driven carousel that maximizes retention, saves, shares, comments, and profile visits.

MAIN GOAL: Create a carousel that feels like a story unfolding, not just a list of information.

CAROUSEL STRUCTURE:
Slide 1 — HOOK / PATTERN INTERRUPT (5-10 words, bold/contrarian/curiosity)
Slide 2 — REHOOK / OPEN LOOP (intrik, jangan reveal dulu)
Slide 3 — RELATABLE PAIN / STORY START ("Most people think…" / "I used to…")
Slides 4–7 — VALUE THROUGH STORY (1 insight per slide, punchy, storytelling)
Slide 8 — TURNING POINT / AHA MOMENT (perspective shift, save-worthy)
Slide 9 — ACTIONABLE TAKEAWAY (steps/framework/checklist)
Slide 10 — CTA / ENGAGEMENT TRIGGER

WRITING STYLE: Short. Sharp. Punchy. Conversational. 1-2 lines per slide. No fluff.

OUTPUT FORMAT:
Slide 1: [copy]
Slide 2: [copy]
...continue until Slide 10.

RULES: Do not explain strategy. No notes unless asked. Not robotic. Not overloaded. Every slide must be swipe-worthy.

TOPIC: [Insert topic]
TARGET AUDIENCE: [Insert audience]
CONTENT GOAL: [Educate / Sell / Build authority / Drive comments / Generate leads]
CTA KEYWORD: [Insert keyword]
```

## Quick Start (untuk Hermest)

Saat diminta bikin carousel, tanya dulu:
1. **Topik** — apa yang ingin disampaikan?
2. **Audience** — siapa pembacanya?
3. **Goal** — edukasi / jual / authority / leads?
4. **CTA keyword** — kata kunci untuk DM/comment

Lalu generate 10 slide langsung tanpa penjelasan tambahan.

## Pitfalls

- Jangan info-dump di satu slide — max 1 insight
- Slide 1 harus bisa berdiri sendiri sebagai standalone hook (buat thumbnail)
- CTA slide 10 harus spesifik, bukan generik "like dan share"
- Open loop slide 2 harus nyambung ke reveal di slide 8
- Bahasa Indonesia: pakai "kamu" bukan "anda" untuk konten personal/healing

## Contoh Implementasi (Ho'oponopono workbook)

**Topic:** Luka pernikahan yang belum sembuh meski sudah memaafkan
**Audience:** Istri 25–45, sudah coba komunikasi/konseling/sabar tapi masih mengganjal
**Goal:** Sell workbook "Peta Luka & Pulih"
**CTA keyword:** PETA

Slide 1: *"Maaf sudah kamu ucapkan. Tapi kenapa dada masih berat?"*
Slide 8 (AHA): *"Karena memaafkan itu urusan pikiran. Menyembuhkan luka itu urusan tubuh."*
Slide 10: *Comment "PETA" dan saya kirim link workbook-nya.*
