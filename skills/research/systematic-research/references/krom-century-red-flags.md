# Krom vs Century: condensed research notes

Use these notes as a starting point for future Indonesian bank-risk comparisons. Re-verify current figures before answering.

## User preference captured in this task class
- User wanted **broader web research**, not an answer constrained to only a few sites.
- For Indonesian bank-risk questions, search in multiple batches across official bank pages, OJK/LPS/BI, IDX/investor-relations PDFs, and reputable financial media.

## Krom public-signal snapshot used in the session
Public 2025/Q1 2026 indicators cited from bank publications/search snippets included:
- Q1 2026 NPL gross around **3.11%**
- Q1 2026 NPL net around **0.16%**
- Q1 2026 total capital / KPMM around **39.78%**
- Q1 2026 LCR around **1,078%**
- March 2026 NSFR around **181%**
- Q1 2026 profitability still positive

Interpretation used in the session:
- These numbers did **not** look like a public-data pattern of imminent collapse.
- Main caveat was **LPS coverage risk**, because advertised deposit rates can exceed the LPS guaranteed-rate cap.

## Century red-flag pattern cited in the session
Numbers and events repeatedly referenced from secondary/summary sources:
- CAR per 31 Oct 2008 around **-3.53%**
- serious liquidity stress in late Oct / early Nov 2008
- **kalah kliring** in mid-Nov 2008
- BI emergency liquidity support / FPJP around **Rp689.4 miliar**
- eventual bailout burden widely cited around **Rp6.7 triliun**

Working comparison logic:
- Century pattern = capital breakdown + liquidity failure + payment failure + emergency support
- If a current bank lacks those public signals, say it is **not currently showing a Century-like public pattern**
- Still separate this from depositor-protection analysis under LPS rules

## Practical phrasing that worked well
- "Dari data publik terbaru, belum terlihat tanda-tanda seperti Century"
- "Sehat ≠ tanpa risiko"
- "Risiko yang lebih relevan untuk nasabah retail di sini adalah bunga di atas batas penjaminan LPS"

## Useful mini-answers from the session
- NPL gross heuristic:
  - <2% strong
  - 2–3% okay
  - 3–5% watchlist
  - >5% red flag
- For user questions like "data kayak gini baru kelihatan per 3 bulan ya?":
  - answer that the cleanest public health snapshot is often **triwulanan**, but some data also appears **bulanan** and **tahunan**.
