# Scorecard Marketing

Lead generation system using interactive quiz/assessment funnels. Converts 30-50% vs 3-10% for traditional PDF lead magnets by creating psychological tension and self-qualification.

Based on Daniel Priestley's "Scorecard Marketing" (ScoreApp methodology serving 150,000+ businesses).

## Why Scorecards Convert Better

- **Curiosity gap**: "Where do I score?" creates irresistible pull
- **Self-qualification**: Responders identify their own gaps
- **Personalization**: Dynamic results feel tailored
- **Low friction**: Answering questions is easier than reading a PDF
- **Data goldmine**: Every answer tells you about the prospect

## Scorecard Design Framework

### Step 1: Choose Your Scorecard Concept

Formula: "[Audience]'s [Topic] Score"

Examples:
- "Your Marketing Maturity Score"
- "Business Scalability Assessment"
- "Health & Wellness Readiness Score"
- "Financial Freedom Score"

**The 3 C's for naming:**
1. **Curious**: Does it create a curiosity gap?
2. **Credible**: Does it sound authoritative?
3. **Clear**: Does it explain what they'll learn?

### Step 2: Design Categories (5 categories ideal)

Each category represents a dimension of performance:

Example for "Marketing Maturity Score":
1. Strategy & Positioning
2. Content & Messaging
3. Lead Generation
4. Conversion Optimization
5. Analytics & Data

### Step 3: Write Assessment Questions

**Rules for good questions:**
- 3 questions per category (15 total is sweet spot)
- Each question has 4-5 answer options scored 1-5
- Questions should be diagnostic, not judgmental
- Start easy, get progressively deeper
- Mix quantitative and qualitative

**Question Templates:**

Type 1 - Frequency:
"How often do you [activity]?"
- Never / Rarely / Sometimes / Often / Always

Type 2 - Agreement:
"[Statement about best practice]"
- Strongly Disagree → Strongly Agree

Type 3 - Stage:
"Which best describes your current [area]?"
- [Stage 1 description] → [Stage 5 description]

Type 4 - Self-Rating:
"On a scale of 1-5, how confident are you in your [area]?"

### Step 4: Create Score Tiers

**3 Tiers (simplest and most effective):**

| Tier | Score Range | Label | Emotion | Next Step |
|------|-----------|-------|---------|-----------|
| Low | 0-40% | "Foundations Needed" | Urgency + Hope | Entry-level offer |
| Medium | 41-70% | "Growth Ready" | Validation + Gap awareness | Core offer |
| High | 71-100% | "Advanced" | Pride + Optimization desire | Premium offer |

### Step 5: Write Dynamic Results

For each tier, provide:

1. **Score Summary**: "You scored X/100 in [Category]"
2. **What It Means**: Interpretation of their score
3. **Quick Wins**: 2-3 immediate actionable tips
4. **The Gap**: What they're missing (creates desire for your solution)
5. **Next Step CTA**: Tailored call-to-action

### Step 6: Landing Page Copy

**Structure:**
1. Headline: "Discover Your [Topic] Score in [X] Minutes"
2. Subheadline: What they'll learn + why it matters
3. Visual: Preview of the assessment/results
4. Bullet points: 3-4 benefits of taking the quiz
5. CTA: "Take the Free Assessment"
6. Social proof: "X people have taken this assessment"
7. Time commitment: "Takes less than 3 minutes"

### Step 7: Follow-Up Sequences

**Email sequence by tier:**

**Low Scorers (Nurture → Educate → Offer)**:
- Email 1: Full results + quick wins
- Email 2: Educational content on their weakest area
- Email 3: Case study of someone who improved
- Email 4: Offer entry-level product/service
- Email 5: Urgency/scarcity

**Medium Scorers (Validate → Challenge → Offer)**:
- Email 1: Full results + what they're doing right
- Email 2: "The gap between good and great"
- Email 3: Specific strategies for their weak areas
- Email 4: Offer core product/service
- Email 5: Testimonial from similar scorer who upgraded

**High Scorers (Celebrate → Optimize → Offer)**:
- Email 1: Full results + congratulations
- Email 2: Advanced strategies others miss
- Email 3: "What the top 1% do differently"
- Email 4: Offer premium/consulting service
- Email 5: Exclusive community/mastermind invite

## Integration with Ads

**Meta Ads → Scorecard Flow:**
1. Ad hooks: "Curious where you stand?" / "Most [audience] score below 50%"
2. Landing page with quiz CTA
3. Quiz completion → Results page with CTA
4. Retarget quiz starters who didn't finish
5. Retarget completers by tier with relevant offers
