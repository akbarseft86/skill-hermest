# Competitive Intelligence + Performance Marketing Research

Use this reference when the user asks for competitor research that must turn into actionable Meta Ads / TikTok / WhatsApp / funnel strategy.

## Scope pattern
Cover both:
1. **Product competitors** — direct brands and indirect substitutes that shape price perception.
2. **Channel/sales competitors** — public pages, agents, consultants, affiliates, creators, landing pages, and ads competing for the same lead intent.

For Coway-like subscription products, direct competitors include rental/subscription/cicilan purifier brands; indirect competitors include galon delivery, depot isi ulang, filter beli putus, RO rumah, dispenser, and low-cost gravity filters.

## Ethical boundaries
- Public sources only.
- Do not enter private groups.
- Do not impersonate buyers.
- Do not collect non-public personal data.
- If individual sales accounts appear publicly, focus on page/account marketing patterns, not personal attacks.
- Use “indikasi winning” when evidence is duration, repetition, creative volume, or repeated angle — do not say “terbukti winning” without performance data.

### E. Price perception ladder
Group findings into:
- Harga beli putus murah
- Harga beli putus menengah
- Harga beli putus premium
- Harga langganan bulanan yang masih bisa diterima
- Batas psikologis yang terasa mahal
- Reasons consumers pay more: service, warranty, convenience, no lifting, brand trust, maintenance, technician visits, lifestyle/peace of mind

## Useful synthesis sections
After tables, always synthesize:
1. 5 positioning gaps not strongly owned by competitors.
2. 3 competitor product ad angles with long-running indications.
3. 3 same-brand sales ad/content angles with long-running indications.
4. Repeated sales patterns: hook, format, CTA, offer, objections answered.
5. Recommendations: Meta angles, TikTok angles, WhatsApp hooks, differentiating content, angles to avoid, low-budget funnel, pre-screening questions.
6. Final sharp conclusion: toughest competitor, cities with visible competition, overcrowded hooks, empty hooks, price framing, realistic positioning, first 14-day plan.

## Quality rules
- Separate fact, estimate, and interpretation.
- Add source URLs for important claims.
- When access is limited by CAPTCHA/login/search backend, state the limitation and mark inference clearly.
- Avoid extreme health claims unless backed by strong source. Prefer cautious language like “membantu kualitas udara lebih terkontrol” over “menyembuhkan alergi”.
- Make output actionable, not just descriptive: every insight should map to ad angle, funnel, WhatsApp script, or lead qualification.

## Proven strategic pattern from Coway session
For high-commitment subscription products (e.g., Rp399rb/month for 84 months), do not frame primarily as “cheap”. Better framing:
- total cost of convenience
- service subscription
- peace of mind
- transparent consultation / audit before selling
- personal cost calculator vs current substitute behavior

A strong low-budget funnel:
1. Organic TikTok/IG Reels answering objections.
2. Boost best content to CTWA.
3. WhatsApp pre-screening before brochure.
4. Mini landing page/FAQ/calculator.
5. Retarget viewers/clickers with service proof and testimonials.

Example WhatsApp pre-screening questions:
- Domisili kota/kecamatan?
- Sekarang pakai galon/depot/filter apa?
- Sebulan habis berapa galon?
- Jumlah penghuni rumah?
- Kebutuhan utama: air minum, udara kamar, atau keduanya?
- Prioritas: hemat, praktis, servis rutin, atau premium lifestyle?
- Budget bulanan nyaman di range berapa?
- Rumah sendiri/kontrak/apartemen?
- Mau dihitungkan skenario biaya sekarang vs opsi produk?
