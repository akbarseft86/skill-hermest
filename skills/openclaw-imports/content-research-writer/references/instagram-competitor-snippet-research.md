# Instagram Competitor Research via Public Snippets

Use this reference when direct Instagram access is blocked/login-gated but the user still needs competitive intelligence from public same-brand sales/agent accounts.

## When to use
- Instagram profile/reels/posts cannot be opened directly due to login, rate limit, CAPTCHA, or HTTP errors.
- Search backends still expose public snippets from Bing/Yahoo/Google/Jina or similar.
- The task is strategy/positioning/funnel analysis, not exact live metric reporting.

## Evidence to extract from snippets
- Profile metrics as ranges or cached facts: followers, following, posts.
- Bio positioning: role/title, brand affiliation, area, official/pro/health planner claims.
- Account type: funnel/product account, personal authority account, or volume/catalogue/keyword-led account.
- CTA/offer words: Gratis Trial, Gratis Diskusi, Katalog, WA, Paket, consultation.
- Sample hooks: e.g. galon pain, udara kotor, hewan peliharaan, alergi/batuk, practical/hygienic, anti-fraud/trust.
- Product names/packages: Neo Plus, Villaem, Ombak, paket 7 tahun, RO, HEPA.
- Engagement examples when shown: likes/comments on sample posts, but treat as directional only.
- Social proof: order/customer posts, awards/rankings, testimonials, anti-fraud language.
- Cross-platform pages: Facebook pages, websites, link-in-bio snippets.

## How to label uncertainty
- Say “public/cached snippet” or “data publik/cache” for metrics.
- Avoid “live account has X followers” unless live IG was verified.
- If multiple snippets disagree, present a range and explain cache age may differ.
- Separate facts from interpretation: “Snippet shows…” vs “Interpretasi marketingnya…”.

## Recommended output shape
1. Access limitation note.
2. Snapshot account: handle, display name, metrics range, bio/positioning.
3. Account archetype: funnel/product, personal authority, volume/catalogue/keyword-led.
4. Strengths: trust, funnel, volume, hooks, proof, SEO handle, consistency.
5. Weaknesses: engagement, product-led confusion, claim risk, lack of decision help, weak funnel clarity.
6. Comparison against other analyzed accounts if available.
7. Tactical gaps: what the user can copy, avoid, and improve.
8. Funnel/WA recommendations: pre-screening questions before brochure/katalog.
9. Sharp conclusion: winning positioning the user can own.

## Coway-specific strategic pattern
For Coway/health-planner accounts, the best differentiator is often not “cheapest” or “best product spec”. Stronger positioning:

> Advisor who helps families calculate whether Coway is actually worth it versus their current galon/depot/filter habit.

Useful CTA:

> “Ketik HITUNG, aku bantu cek apakah Coway masuk akal buat rumahmu atau belum.”

Use pre-screening before brochure:
- Domisili/kecamatan?
- Sekarang pakai galon/depot/filter apa?
- Sebulan habis berapa galon?
- Jumlah penghuni rumah?
- Kebutuhan utama: air minum, udara kamar, atau keduanya?
- Prioritas: hemat, praktis, servis rutin, atau premium lifestyle?
- Budget bulanan nyaman di range berapa?
- Rumah sendiri/kontrak/apartemen?
- Mau dihitungkan skenario biaya sekarang vs opsi produk?

## Health-claim caution
Avoid saying or implying that purifier/air purifier cures illness. Prefer cautious wording:
- “membantu kualitas udara lebih terkontrol”
- “membantu mengurangi partikel tertentu”
- “lebih nyaman untuk rumah dengan debu/hewan”
- “bukan klaim penyembuhan alergi/batuk”
