# StoryBrand Messaging

Framework for clarifying your message so customers will listen. Positions your customer as the hero and your brand as the guide in a story structure that resonates.

Based on Donald Miller's "Building a StoryBrand" — used by 10,000+ businesses from startups to Fortune 500.

## The 7-Part StoryBrand Framework (SB7)

Every great story follows this pattern. Your marketing should too:

### 1. A CHARACTER (Your Customer)
- Who is the hero? (NOT your brand)
- What do they want? (Be specific)
- Example: "A busy professional who wants to eat healthy without meal prep"

### 2. HAS A PROBLEM
Three levels of problem:
- **External**: The tangible, surface-level problem
  - "I don't have time to cook healthy meals"
- **Internal**: How it makes them FEEL
  - "I feel guilty about my eating habits"
- **Philosophical**: Why it's just plain WRONG
  - "People shouldn't have to choose between health and their career"

**The villain** causing the problem:
- Name a specific villain (not your customer)
- Example: "The 'hustle culture' that normalizes skipping meals"

### 3. AND MEETS A GUIDE (Your Brand)
Position yourself as the guide with:
- **Empathy**: "We understand what it's like to..."
- **Authority**: Credentials that prove you can help
  - Testimonials, statistics, awards, logos, years of experience

### 4. WHO GIVES THEM A PLAN
Create a simple 3-step plan:
1. Step 1: [Easy first step] — e.g., "Choose your meals"
2. Step 2: [Core action] — e.g., "We deliver to your door"
3. Step 3: [Desired outcome] — e.g., "Enjoy healthy eating on autopilot"

Two types of plans:
- **Process Plan**: Steps to buy/use your product
- **Agreement Plan**: Promises that remove fear (guarantee, easy cancel)

### 5. AND CALLS THEM TO ACTION
Two types:
- **Direct CTA**: "Buy Now", "Schedule a Call", "Start Free Trial"
  - Must be clear, visible, repeated
- **Transitional CTA**: "Download Free Guide", "Watch Demo"
  - For people not ready to buy yet

### 6. THAT HELPS THEM AVOID FAILURE
Paint the picture of what happens if they DON'T act:
- What are the negative consequences?
- What will they lose?
- Don't overdo it — just enough to motivate

### 7. AND ENDS IN SUCCESS
Paint the picture of transformation:
- **Status**: How will others see them?
- **Self-acceptance**: How will they feel about themselves?
- **Self-transcendence**: How will they contribute to something bigger?

## Brand Script Template

Fill in this template for any product/brand:

```
CHARACTER: [Who is your customer and what do they want?]

PROBLEM:
- External: [Tangible problem]
- Internal: [How it makes them feel]
- Philosophical: [Why it's wrong]
- Villain: [What/who causes this]

GUIDE:
- Empathy: [Show you understand]
- Authority: [Prove you can help]

PLAN:
1. [Step 1]
2. [Step 2]
3. [Step 3]

CALL TO ACTION:
- Direct: [Primary CTA]
- Transitional: [Lead magnet CTA]

FAILURE: [What happens if they don't act]

SUCCESS:
- Status: [How others see them]
- Self-acceptance: [How they feel]
- Self-transcendence: [Bigger impact]
```

## One-Liner Formula

Structure: Problem + Solution + Result

Template: "We help [CHARACTER] who struggle with [PROBLEM] achieve [SUCCESS] through [PLAN/SOLUTION]."

## Website Wireframe (StoryBrand)

1. **Header**: Headline (what you offer) + CTA + Image showing success
2. **Stakes**: What's at risk if they don't solve this
3. **Value Proposition**: Brief benefits (not features)
4. **Guide**: Empathy statement + Authority proof
5. **Plan**: Your simple 3-step process
6. **Explanatory Paragraph**: Expand on your solution
7. **Video** (optional): Show/explain your product
8. **Price Choices** (if applicable)
9. **Junk Drawer**: Footer with all other links

## Key Rules

1. **NEVER position your brand as the hero** — Your customer is the hero
2. **Talk about their problem, not your solution** first
3. **Keep it simple** — If you confuse, you lose
4. **Repeat your CTA** at least 3 times on a page
5. **Show both failure AND success** — Stakes + aspiration
6. **One message per page** — Don't dilute
7. **Use their language** — Not industry jargon
