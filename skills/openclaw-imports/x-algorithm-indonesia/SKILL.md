## CORE PRINCIPLE: How X Ranking Actually Works

X uses a multi-model AI system called **Phoenix** (a Grok-based transformer) with a three-stage pipeline. The algorithm has eliminated all hand-engineered features — the machine learns and decides entirely.

### Stage 1 — Candidate Pool (~1,500 posts per feed refresh)
The system pulls from two sources:
- **In-network (Thunder)**: Real-time posts from accounts you follow (sub-millisecond lookups)
- **Out-of-network (Phoenix Retrieval)**: ML-discovered content from accounts you don't follow via two-tower neural network embeddings

**This means your content can outrank posts from followed accounts if the algorithm predicts higher engagement.**

### Stage 2 — Signal Scoring (15+ Predicted Actions)
Phoenix predicts probabilities across 15+ user actions, combined with weighted scoring:

| Signal | Weight | What It Means |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| Replies to replies (nested threads) | **HIGHEST (~75× multiplier vs like)** | Deep conversation = algorithm gold |
| Direct replies | **HIGH (~13.5-27× multiplier)** | Conversation starter signal |
| Reposts / Retweets | **HIGH (20× multiplier)** | Amplification = quality endorsement |
| Quote tweets | **HIGH (15× multiplier)** | Added perspective = high value |
| Profile clicks | **MEDIUM-HIGH (12× multiplier)** | Curiosity about author = strong signal |
| Link clicks | **MEDIUM (11× multiplier)** | Intent to explore further |
| Bookmarks/Saves | **MEDIUM (10× multiplier)** | "I want to return to this" |
| Likes | **BASELINE (1× multiplier)** | Necessary but not sufficient |
| **Negative: Block/Mute** | **-1000× penalty** | Severe reputation damage |
| **Negative: "Not interested"** | **-100× penalty** | Strong negative signal |
| **Negative: Report** | **Manual review trigger** | Potential account restriction |

**Final Score = Σ (weight × probability of action)**

### Stage 3 — Heuristics, Filters & Delivery
- **Visibility filtering**: Blocked/muted accounts removed
- **Author diversity**: Prevents same account dominating feed
- **Content balance**: Mix of in-network and out-of-network
- **Feedback-based fatigue**: Negative feedback reduces ranking
- **Social proof**: Posts engaged by followed accounts get boost

## ENGAGEMENT VELOCITY: The #1 Ranking Factor

**Rate of engagement beats total volume.** The first 30-60 minutes after posting determine your entire distribution trajectory.

The algorithm tests each tweet with a small initial audience:
- **Above-average early engagement** → progressively shown to larger audiences (snowball effect)
- **Dormant first hour** → deprioritized and buried

**Everything in this skill — timing, reply speed, hooks — is designed to maximize that first-hour velocity.**

## TWEEPCRED: The Hidden Authority Score

TweepCred is X's hidden reputation system (0-100 scale) that determines distribution eligibility:

| TweepCred Score | Impact |
|-----------------|--------|
| **Below 0.65** | Algorithm considers only 3 of your tweets for distribution |
| **Above 0.65** | All tweets eligible for algorithmic distribution |
| **Higher scores** | Priority placement in For You feed |

### Factors Affecting TweepCred:
- Account age and consistency
- Follower count and follower-to-following ratio
- Engagement quality (not just quantity)
- Device usage patterns (human-like behavior)
- Content originality (duplicate detection penalizes templates)
- Historical violations (time-decayed but stack within 90 days)

## CONTENT OPTIMIZATION

### Character Count
- **Under 280 characters** → baseline for single tweets
- **Premium accounts**: Up to 4,000 characters (2×-4× visibility boost)
- **Thread posts**: Keep individual tweets punchy, 100-200 characters optimal
- **Scannability hack**: Line breaks, bullet points, and emoji breaks improve readability

### Content Format Priority (Algorithm Weight)

| Format | Exposure Multiplier | Best For |
|--------|---------------------|----------|
| **Native video** | **5× base exposure** | Highest engagement, keep under 2:20 |
| **Images/Photos** | **3× base exposure** | Visual storytelling, infographics |
| **Polls** | **High engagement** | Easy one-click interaction |
| **Text-only** | **1× base exposure** | Quick takes, hot opinions |
| **External links (non-Premium)** | **Near-zero visibility** | Avoid unless Premium subscriber |

**Rule: Native video dominates. X wants to keep users ON the platform.**

### Link Strategy (Critical 2025 Update)

| Account Type | Link Post Visibility |
|--------------|---------------------|
| **Non-Premium** | Near-zero exposure (effectively invisible) |
| **Premium ($8/month)** | Reduced but viable (~0.25-0.3% engagement) |

**Link Workarounds:**
1. **Place links in replies** to your own post (not the main tweet)
2. **Put links in final thread tweet** (minimize main post penalty)
3. **Use Linktree in bio** and reference "link in bio" in posts
4. **Subscribe to Premium** if driving traffic is core to your strategy

## THREAD STRUCTURE

### The 5-7-3 Formula (for deep content)
- Posts 1-2: Hook and promise
- Posts 3-7: Core content and value delivery
- Posts 8-10: Evidence, examples, data
- Final post: Conclusion and CTA
- **Total: 10 tweets maximum for optimal completion**

### Thread Length Performance

| Length | Completion Rate | Best For | Algorithm Treatment |
|--------|----------------|----------|-------------------|
| 3-5 tweets | 85-90% | Quick tips, hot takes | Favored for velocity |
| 5-7 tweets | 70-80% | Standard educational | **Optimal balance** |
| 8-10 tweets | 50-65% | Deep dives, case studies | Medium reach |
| 10-15 tweets | 35-50% | Comprehensive guides | Lower completion but high saves |
| 15+ tweets | <30% | Avoid | Significant drop-off |

### Thread Formatting Best Practices

**Numbering:**
- Use "1/7", "2/7" format at start or end of each tweet
- Sets expectations and helps readers track progress

**Visual Breaks:**
- Add relevant image every 2-3 tweets
- Use line breaks generously (1-2 between ideas)
- Bullet points (• or —) for scannability

**Engagement Checkpoints:**
- **Tweet 3**: Ask rhetorical question or surprising fact
- **Tweet 5**: Personal story or relatable example
- **Tweet 7**: Direct question to drive replies
- **Final tweet**: Clear CTA with value proposition

### Default Recommendation
When unsure, use **5-7 tweets**. It's the sweet spot balancing completion rate with depth.

## REPLY STRATEGY (The Highest-Impact Growth Lever)

Replying strategically is one of the most powerful growth tactics on X. The "Reply Guy" strategy can 10x-100x impressions for small accounts.

### Reply Timing Playbook

| Timeframe | Action | Impact |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| 0-15 min | Reply to every comment immediately | Maximum velocity boost |
| 15-30 min | Continue replying, ask follow-up questions | Sustained distribution |
| 30-60 min | Reply to your OWN post with additional context | Re-engagement trigger |
| 2-24 hours | Periodic check-ins for new comments | Long-tail engagement |

### Why Replies Matter So Much
- **Replies to replies get ~75× multiplier** vs a standard like
- **Direct replies get ~13.5-27× multiplier** vs a like
- Nested conversation threads signal high-quality content
- Replies insert you into the "correct data bucket" for algorithm categorization

### The Strategic Reply Growth Strategy

**For accounts under 5,000 followers:**
- Post 50-90% of content to Communities (not just timeline)
- Aim for **50-100 thoughtful replies per day** on relevant accounts
- Target: High-relevance posts in your niche from larger accounts
- Focus on adding genuine value, not generic responses

**Reply Quality Hierarchy:**
1. **Insightful additions** → Build authority, attract followers
2. **Thoughtful questions** → Drive conversation, get visibility
3. **Personal experiences** → Create connection, encourage engagement
4. **Wit/humor** → Viral potential, shareability
5. **Generic responses** → Ignored or penalized as low-effort

**Practical rule: For every 1 post you publish, aim for 10-20 thoughtful replies on other creators' content.**

## HASHTAG STRATEGY

Unlike Threads, X uses traditional hashtags — but with evolved best practices for 2025.

### Rules
- **1-2 hashtags per post maximum** — more looks spammy
- **Place hashtags at end** of tweet (not inline) for readability
- **Research trending hashtags** in your niche
- **Create branded hashtags** for series content

### Best Practices
1. **Specific beats generic** — #IndonesiaStartup beats #Business
2. **Mix trending + niche** — broader discovery + targeted reach
3. **Avoid banned/problematic hashtags** — can trigger shadowban
4. **Hashtags in first tweet of thread** only (not throughout)

## SHADOWBAN DETECTION & RECOVERY

### 5-Step Detection Test
1. **Search Visibility**: Log out → search your username and latest post text → if invisible = possible restriction
2. **Engagement Ratio**: Post neutral content → if near-zero impressions after 12-24h = restriction likely
3. **Audience Check**: Ask 3-5 followers if they see your posts in For You → if only via profile = throttled
4. **New Account Comparison**: Post similar content from fresh account → if 5×+ reach = flagged
5. **Comment Visibility**: Comment on trending post → if buried/hidden = strong restriction sign

### Recovery Protocol

| Phase | Duration | Action |
|-------|----------|--------|
| Full stop | 24-48 hours | Stop ALL posting, liking, replying |
| Content audit | Day 1-2 | Delete spammy or problematic content |
| Appeal | Day 1 | Submit support ticket with evidence |
| Safe period | Day 3-7 | Post ONLY safe, informational content |
| Gradual return | Week 2+ | Resume normal activity slowly |

### Duration Expectations
- Minor violations: 48-72 hours
- Moderate issues: 7-14 days
- Repeat offenses: 30+ days or permanent

### 2025 Algorithm Shifts to Avoid
- **Heavier media hashing**: Duplicate images/videos trip safety systems faster
- **Topic-aware rate limits**: News topics have stricter caps for new accounts
- **Time-decayed reputation**: Past violations fade, but repeated offenses stack aggressively
- **Cross-signal consistency**: Posting cadence, DM volume, login geography blend into trust score

## EDIT/DELETE RULES

- **Edit button (Premium)**: Edit freely within 30 minutes — minimal algorithmic impact
- **Major content rewrites**: May trigger re-evaluation
- **DELETE-REPOST**: ⚠️ AVOID — duplicate content detection and artificial velocity patterns mean reposted content almost always performs worse

**If a post underperforms**: Do NOT delete-repost. Instead, repurpose with a completely new angle and hook. Wait at least 24 hours.

When asked to write X content, create a posting schedule, or advise on growth strategy, ALWAYS apply these rules:
