# $100M Offers Framework

Create offers so good people feel stupid saying no. Based on Alex Hormozi's "$100M Offers" — the framework that has helped thousands of entrepreneurs restructure their pricing and packaging.

## The Value Equation

**Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood of Achievement) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)**

To increase value, you must:
- ↑ **Dream Outcome**: Make the end result bigger/better
- ↑ **Perceived Likelihood**: Increase their confidence it'll work
- ↓ **Time Delay**: Make results happen faster
- ↓ **Effort & Sacrifice**: Make it easier for them

## Grand Slam Offer Creation Process

### Step 1: Identify the Dream Outcome

Questions to ask:
- What does your customer want MORE than anything?
- What would their ideal "after" state look like?
- If you could wave a magic wand, what would change?
- What outcome would they pay almost anything for?

**Be specific**: Not "lose weight" but "lose 15kg in 90 days while still eating foods you love"

### Step 2: List All Problems & Obstacles

Brain dump EVERY problem between their current state and dream outcome:

For each problem, think about:
- What happens immediately before?
- What happens immediately after?
- What are the physical/tangible obstacles?
- What are the emotional/psychological obstacles?
- What are the social obstacles?

### Step 3: Create Solutions for Each Problem

For EVERY problem listed, create a solution. Solutions can be:
- **Done-for-you (DFY)**: You do it for them (highest value)
- **Done-with-you (DWY)**: You help them do it (medium value)
- **Do-it-yourself (DIY)**: Tools/templates they use alone (lowest value)

### Step 4: Stack Delivery Vehicles

Trim and stack solutions into delivery vehicles:

| Vehicle | Examples | Value Perception |
|---------|----------|-----------------|
| 1-on-1 | Coaching calls, consulting | Very High |
| Small group | Mastermind, group coaching | High |
| Course/Training | Video modules, workshops | Medium |
| Tools/Templates | Spreadsheets, scripts, swipe files | Medium |
| Community | Private group, forum | Medium |
| Software/Tech | Apps, automations | High |
| Physical | Books, kits, devices | Variable |

### Step 5: Enhance with Bonuses

**Bonus stacking rules:**
1. Each bonus should solve a specific objection
2. Name each bonus with a benefit-driven title
3. Assign a dollar value to each bonus
4. Bonuses should complement, not duplicate, the core offer
5. Create urgency: "Available only for the first X customers"

**Bonus naming formula**: [Specific Result] + [Timeframe] + [Method]
- "The 7-Day Content Calendar That Generates 30 Days of Posts"
- "The 5-Minute Lead Magnet Template (Converts at 45%)"

### Step 6: Design the Guarantee

Types of guarantees (from weak to strong):
1. **Conditional**: "If you do X, Y, Z and don't get results..."
2. **Unconditional**: "No questions asked refund within X days"
3. **Anti-guarantee**: "All sales final" (works for premium/scarce offers)
4. **Performance**: "If you don't get [result], we'll [specific remedy]"
5. **Outrageous**: "If you don't [result], I'll [extreme action]"

**Stack guarantees**: Combine conditional + unconditional for maximum power

### Step 7: Name the Offer (MAGIC Formula)

**M** — Make a Magnetic Reason Why
**A** — Announce Your Avatar
**G** — Give Them a Goal
**I** — Indicate a Time Interval
**C** — Complete with a Container Word

Examples:
- "The 90-Day Digital Marketing Mastery Accelerator"
- "The 6-Week Revenue Explosion Blueprint"
- "The 30-Day Content Machine Challenge"

**Container words**: Challenge, Blueprint, Accelerator, Bootcamp, System, Formula, Masterclass, Intensive, Sprint, Protocol

### Step 8: Price for Value (Not Cost)

**Pricing principles:**
- Price based on the VALUE of the outcome, not cost to deliver
- Higher prices = more committed customers = better results
- The "10x rule": Your offer should deliver 10x the price in value
- Don't compete on price — compete on value

**Price anchoring technique:**
1. State the real value of everything included
2. Show what similar outcomes cost elsewhere
3. Reveal your price (much lower than perceived value)
4. Add bonuses to tip the scales

## The Offer Audit Checklist

Rate your offer 1-10 on each:

- [ ] Dream Outcome: Is the end result clearly desirable? 
- [ ] Specificity: Are results measurable and timebound?
- [ ] Likelihood: Do prospects believe they'll achieve it?
- [ ] Speed: How fast do they see results?
- [ ] Effort: How easy is it for them?
- [ ] Bonuses: Do they address key objections?
- [ ] Guarantee: Does it reverse all risk?
- [ ] Naming: Does the name create desire?
- [ ] Pricing: Is perceived value 10x the price?
- [ ] Scarcity: Is there a real reason to act now?
