## CORE PRINCIPLE: How Threads Ranking Actually Works

Threads uses a multi-model AI system (not a single algorithm) with a three-stage pipeline:

### Stage 1 — Candidate Pool (~2,000 posts per feed refresh)
The system pulls from: followed accounts, mutuals, accounts interacted with in last 14 days, and users who participate in the same reply threads (conversation-aligned discovery). **This means your reply behavior directly determines who discovers you.**

### Stage 2 — Signal Scoring
Four signal families, ranked by weight:

| Signal | Weight | What It Means |
|--------|--------|---------------|
| Reply depth (reply-to-reply chains) | HIGHEST (3.2× multiplier vs top-level like) | Nested conversations are gold |
| Reposts / Quote posts | HIGH | Amplification signals |
| Time spent on post (dwell time) | MEDIUM-HIGH | Readers who linger = quality signal |
| Saves / Bookmarks | MEDIUM (stronger than likes) | "I want to come back to this" |
| Profile clicks | MEDIUM | Curiosity about the author = ranking boost |
| Likes | LOW (baseline only) | Necessary but not sufficient |

### Stage 3 — Diversification & Delivery
- **Creator cap**: Max ~1 post per scroll session regardless of score — posting 10x/day won't flood feeds
- **Bridge content**: ~30% of each feed comes from creators the user hasn't engaged with in 72+ hours — this is how new audiences discover you
- **Geo-cohort clustering**: Content is pushed during high-activity windows in the user's timezone — this is why posting at Indonesia peak times matters enormously

## CONTENT OPTIMIZATION

### Character Count
- **Under 500 characters → 19% ranking lift** (confirmed across multiple analyses)
- Over 1,200 characters → deprioritized unless it contains scannability markers
- **Scannability hack**: Posts with 2+ question marks or emoji breaks can bypass length penalties on longer posts
- When writing posts: front-load the hook in the first line, keep it punchy, break long thoughts into thread posts instead of one wall of text

### Content Format Priority
1. **Image + conversational text** → highest engagement (+60% vs text-only)
2. **Video/carousel + text** → strong performance (official Threads guidance: "posts that include text perform better than those without any text")
3. **Text with link** → +17% vs text-only (link penalty is officially debunked as of 2025)
4. **Text-only** → baseline

**Rule: Always pair media with descriptive, conversational text. Never post a naked image or video without copy.**

### Link Strategy (Updated 2025)
- **Links are NOT penalized** — Adam Mosseri confirmed Threads adjusted ranking to assign greater value to posts with URLs
- Links now perform **+17% better** than text-only posts
- Links still underperform images (+60%) — so pair links WITH images when possible
- For Shopee/affiliate links: include link in first post for maximum visibility, or tease value before placing in final post
- No platform discrimination against affiliate links on Threads

## THREAD STRUCTURE

### The 5-7-3 Formula (for deep content)
- Posts 1–5: Setup and main argument
- Posts 6–12: Evidence and examples
- Posts 13–15: Conclusion and CTA
- Total: 15 posts maximum

### Thread Length Performance

| Length | Completion Rate | Best For | Algorithm Treatment |
|--------|----------------|----------|-------------------|
| 3–5 posts | 85–90% | Quick tips, hot takes | Favored for velocity |
| 5–7 posts | 70–80% | Standard educational | **Optimal balance** |
| 8–12 posts | 50–65% | Deep dives, case studies | Medium |
| 12–15 posts | 35–50% | Comprehensive guides | Lower reach but high save rate |
| 15+ posts | <30% | Avoid | Significant drop-off |

### Engagement Checkpoints Within Threads
- **Post 3**: Ask a rhetorical question → hooks continuation
- **Post 7**: Mini-cliffhanger or surprising data point → maintains interest
- **Post 10**: Direct question or poll prompt → drives replies
- **Final post**: Clear CTA with value proposition → converts engagement

### Default Recommendation
When unsure, use **5–7 posts**. It's the sweet spot balancing completion rate with depth.

## REPLY STRATEGY (The Highest-Impact Growth Lever)

Replying to comments within 15–30 minutes drives a **+42% engagement lift** on Threads — the highest among all major platforms.

### Reply Timing Playbook

| Timeframe | Action | Impact |
|-----------|--------|--------|
| 0–15 min | Reply to every comment immediately | Maximum velocity boost |
| 15–30 min | Continue replying, ask follow-up questions | Sustained distribution |
| 30–60 min | Reply to your OWN post with additional context/value | Re-engagement trigger |
| 2–24 hours | Periodic check-ins for new comments | Long-tail engagement |

### Why This Works
- Replies count as engagement, extending the post's algorithmic life
- Nested replies (reply-to-reply) get a **3.2× multiplier** compared to top-level likes
- The system analyzes full conversation topology — deep threads signal quality
- "Replies account for almost half of the views on Threads" — Meta Creator Education Hub

### The Reply-First Growth Strategy
"If you're really trying to grow your presence, you should reply much more than you post. And the sum of all your replies is about as valuable as the sum of all the value of all your posts." — Adam Mosseri

**Practical rule: For every 1 post you publish, aim for 5–10 thoughtful replies on other creators' content.**

## TOPIC TAGS (Not Hashtags)

Threads uses a fundamentally different system than Instagram or X:

### Rules
- **ONE tag per post only** — the system enforces this limit
- Tags can include spaces and phrases (e.g., "AI Productivity Tips")
- No # symbol required — tags appear as blue labels
- Multi-word expressions are valid and preferred over generic single words

### Best Practices
1. Choose the **single most specific and relevant tag** — "Indonesia AI Training" beats "AI"
2. Use phrase-based tags — more specific = better discovery
3. Check trending topics — Threads shows active user counts per topic
4. Add up to 10 topics to your profile bio — helps the algorithm categorize your content
5. **Don't rely on tags alone** — posts with strong conversation starters ranked 40% higher than tag-reliant posts

## SAVE/BOOKMARK OPTIMIZATION

Saves carry more weight than likes in the ranking hierarchy. To maximize save signals:

1. Create **reference material** — tips lists, resource compilations, step-by-step guides
2. Longer threads (12–15 posts) accumulate more saves even with lower completion rates
3. Educational content dramatically outperforms ephemeral content on save metrics
4. Include a clear reason WHY someone should save: "Save this for your next campaign" or "Bookmark this framework"
5. "Save-worthy" content gets long-term algorithmic visibility beyond the first-hour velocity window

## SHADOWBAN DETECTION & RECOVERY

### 5-Step Detection Test
1. **Search Visibility**: Log out → search your username and latest post text → if invisible = possible ban
2. **Engagement Ratio**: Post neutral content → if near-zero impressions after 12–24h = restriction likely
3. **Audience Check**: Ask 3–5 followers if they see your posts in For You → if only via profile = throttled
4. **New Account Comparison**: Post similar content from fresh account → if 5×+ reach = flagged
5. **Comment Visibility**: Comment on trending post → if buried/hidden = strong ban sign

### Recovery Protocol

| Phase | Duration | Action |
|-------|----------|--------|
| Full stop | 24–48 hours | Stop ALL posting, liking, replying |
| Content audit | Day 1–2 | Delete spammy or problematic content |
| Appeal | Day 1 | Settings → Help → Report a Problem |
| Safe period | Day 3–7 | Post ONLY safe, informational content |
| Gradual return | Week 2+ | Resume normal activity slowly |

### Duration Expectations
- Minor violations: 48–72 hours
- Moderate issues: 7–14 days
- Repeat offenses: 30+ days or permanent

### Account Status Dashboard (Available since May 2025)
Check: Settings → Account Status → Shows recommendation eligibility, policy violations, demotion notifications

## EDIT/DELETE RULES

- **Typo corrections**: Edit freely — minimal to no algorithmic impact
- **Adding/removing tags**: Resets your position in that tag's feed
- **Major content rewrites**: May trigger re-evaluation
- **DELETE-REPOST**: ⚠️ AVOID — duplicate content detection, artificial velocity patterns, and potential spam flags mean reposted content almost always performs worse

**If a post underperforms**: Do NOT delete-repost. Instead, repurpose with a completely new angle and hook. Wait at least 24 hours.
